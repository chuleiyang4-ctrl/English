ENGLISH · LESSON 4.2

Writing & AI-Assisted Editing

Effective AI collaboration isn't hitting "generate" and hoping — it's a clear division of labor. You architect the intent and logic; AI compiles it into polished, idiomatic English.

Practice method · Workplace Academic

I. Human-in-the-Loop: Who Does What

Think of it as an architect briefing a compiler — not a vague request to a black box.

🧠 You — the Architect
  • Supply a dense draft of your core intent
  • Set the context and tone
  • Do the final fact-check and quality control
🤖 AI — the Compiler
  • Grammar checking and idiom correction
  • Elegant sentence restructuring
  • Adapting length and tone across formats

II. A Reusable Polishing Prompt

A constrained prompt template gets far more consistent, idiomatic output than an open-ended "make this better."

# Role & Task
Act as an expert native English editor. Polish the following draft for a [tech doc / pitch email / GitHub PR].

# Constraints
1. Keep the tone [professional yet concise].
2. Preserve the original core logic.
3. Highlight the key changes and briefly explain why.

# Draft input
"[paste your raw draft — rough notes or mixed-language logic are fine]"

III. The 3-Step Writing Loop

A loop, not a one-shot request — each round feeds directly into the next draft.

1
Write a bullet-point draft●

Don't worry about grammar or spelling — just list the core logic quickly, in plain words or fragments.

2
AI restructuring & tone pass●

Feed in the prompt template above and ask for 2–3 tonal variants — formal, casual, concise.

3
Diff review and absorb●●

Compare your draft against the AI's version. Notice which collocations it swapped in — that's the part worth feeding back into your own vocabulary.

Practice

Your bullet draft reads: "tell client delay, not our fault, vendor issue, new date next fri." Using the Architect/Compiler split, what should you decide before handing this to AI, versus what should the AI decide?

Show answer

You (Architect) decide: the tone (apologetic but firm, not defensive), which facts must stay (vendor caused it, new date is next Friday), and who the audience is (a client, so keep it professional). AI (Compiler) decides: the exact sentence structure, connecting words, and phrasing that sounds natural and polished in English — the surface form, not the substance.
